# Caption Way

**Caption Way** is a Chrome extension that helps you **capture live captions from Google Meet** and export them into a clean, professional **PDF transcript**.  
It’s designed for students, professionals, and educators who want an easy way to save meeting notes.

---

## ✨ Features
- 🎤 **Live Caption Capture** – Records captions directly from Google Meet in real time.  
- 📄 **Export to PDF** – Save transcripts with clean formatting, bold session headers, and highlighted important lines.  
- 🎯 **Smart Keyword Detection** – Identifies top academic keywords from your sessions.  
- 📺 **Suggested Learning Resources** – Adds YouTube links for related academic topics.  
- ⚡ **Lightweight & Secure** – Works entirely in your browser; no external servers, no data collection.

---

## 🚀 How to Use
1. Install **Caption Way** from the Chrome Web Store.  
2. Open a Google Meet and **turn on captions**.  
3. Click the extension icon → **Start** to begin capturing.  
4. Click **Stop** when finished.  
5. Export your transcript with **Download PDF**.  

---

## 🔒 Privacy
Caption Way **does not collect, store, or transmit your data**.  
All processing happens locally on your computer.
